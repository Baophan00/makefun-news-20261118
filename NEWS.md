# World News — 18 November 2026

A shortlist of eight consequential world stories, stated as facts.

1. Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen at APEC ahead of a trilateral summit with Donald Trump. Xi urged free and open trade as the US restricted China's access to advanced AI chips.

2. Iran said it attacked US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain. Bahrain intercepted the attacks. US aircraft struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm island. Shipping through Hormuz was disrupted and an ADNOC tanker crew member went missing.

3. Ukraine destroyed the Kerch road bridge linking Crimea to Russia.

4. The Quad held its first leaders' meeting in over two years in Manila, with Trump, Albanese, Modi and Takaichi. The US and Philippines announced a trade deal.

5. Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican Senator Dan Sullivan in Alaska, giving Democrats a 56-44 Senate majority.

6. Nvidia reported Q3 revenue of 115.84 billion dollars, doubled year on year and beat forecasts. Its board said it would not reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO.

7. Major outages hit Microsoft, the FAA, banks, Apple's App Store and Gmail. US officials said there was no evidence of a systemic cyberattack.

8. Gold fell below 3,800 dollars and the dollar held near a 19-month high as markets priced more Fed hikes.
