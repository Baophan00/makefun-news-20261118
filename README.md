A deterministic 1024x1024 poster of six stories from 18 November 2026: Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen at APEC before a trilateral summit with Donald Trump; Iran says it struck US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain as the US hit a missile site on Qeshm island and Hormuz shipping was disrupted; Ukraine destroys the Kerch road bridge; the Quad meets in Manila for the first time in over two years; Mary Peltola wins the Alaska Senate seat, giving Democrats a 56-44 majority; and Nvidia reports 115.84 billion dollars in Q3 revenue alongside major tech outages and gold below 3,800 dollars.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`entry.png` is copied to `/output/entry.png` and the copy is verified byte for byte.
